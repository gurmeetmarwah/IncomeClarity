About IncomeClarityLab

IncomeClarityLab builds practical US-only financial calculators for people who want clearer numbers before making decisions.

We focus on simple interfaces, transparent assumptions, and tools that are fast to use on desktop or mobile. Our calculators are designed to help with income planning, debt payoff strategy, and major trade-offs like renting versus buying.

Every calculator is educational and should be used for planning. For legal, tax, or investment advice, consult a licensed professional.
