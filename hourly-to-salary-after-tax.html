Popular Income Questions (FAQ)

Is $25/hour good in the US?

$25/hour can be solid in lower-cost areas, but affordability depends on taxes, rent, debt, and local prices. Use the calculator above to estimate your actual take-home first.

How much rent can you afford on $25/hour?

A common guideline is to keep rent around 30% of monthly take-home pay. On $25/hour, your workable rent budget changes by state taxes and weekly hours worked.

$30/hour is how much per year?

At 40 hours per week, $30/hour is about $62,400 gross yearly before taxes.

How much do taxes take from hourly pay?

Most workers see federal income tax, Social Security, Medicare, and state income tax withholding. The exact share changes by pay level and state.
