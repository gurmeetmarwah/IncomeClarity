"$15, $20, $30, $40 an hour is how much a year?"

There is a simple formula. Take your hourly rate. Multiply by your weekly hours. Then by 52 weeks. That is your gross yearly pay. But that is not what hits your bank. To get your take-home, you need to take out tax. Federal, FICA, and state. The cards below show you all four common rates. With both gross pay and a rough monthly take-home.

$15/hour is how much a year?

$15/hour = $31,200/year

Before tax · full-time (40 hrs/week × 52 weeks)

After tax (US average): ~$2,050/month

Details

Yearly pay before tax

At $15 an hour with a 40-hour week, the math is $15 × 40 × 52 = $31,200 per year. That is your gross pay. The number a job ad shows you.

Monthly pay before tax

Divide the year by 12. That is about $2,600 a month before tax. Most US jobs pay every two weeks. So you get 26 checks a year. Each one is roughly half your monthly gross.

What you actually take home

Tax cuts your real pay. Federal tax, Social Security, Medicare, and state tax all come out. The $2,050 a month above is the US average. Your state can move this a lot. Use the calculator at the top of this page for your real number.

Who earns this rate

Many entry-level hourly jobs sit near $15. Your rent and city matter more than the gross pay alone. So always pair this benchmark with a real after-tax estimate.

$20/hour = $41,600/year

Before tax · full-time (40 hrs/week × 52 weeks)

After tax (US average): ~$2,650/month

Details

Yearly pay before tax

Same full-time week. $20 × 40 × 52 = $41,600 a year. Gross. That is the simple answer when you want a yearly number from a posted hourly rate.

Monthly pay before tax

Divide by 12. That is about $3,467 a month. If you are comparing job offers, check the real weekly hours. Overtime or uneven shifts shift the yearly total.

Your take-home depends on your state

Your real after-tax pay depends on your state's tax rules. The $2,650 a month above is a US average. Put your state in our calculator for your real number.

Practical context

$20 an hour is a lower-to-middle income in most cities. Two people at $20 an hour can feel very different. It depends on rent, debt, and commute.

Yearly pay before tax

Lifestyle band

In most US cities, $30 an hour supports a middle-income budget. As long as your rent stays in line. But high rent or big debt can squeeze you. So always check your real after-tax pay.

Popular Income Questions (FAQ)

What is an hourly to salary calculator?

A tool that turns your hourly pay into yearly and monthly numbers. Gross first. Then take-home after tax. You enter your rate, your weekly hours, and your state. It uses real US tax rules to give you a real number. Helpful when you compare two job offers. Or when you want to compare a freelance rate to a salary.

How do I turn an hourly wage into a yearly salary?

Multiply three numbers. Your hourly rate. Your weekly hours. And 52 weeks. So yearly gross = hourly × hours per week × 52. Most full-time jobs are 40 hours a week. If your hours change a lot, use your real average. Not 40.

How do I turn hourly pay into a monthly salary?

First get your yearly gross (hourly × hours × 52). Then divide by 12. That is your monthly gross. If you get paid every two weeks, your monthly check won't match this exactly. Some months you get three checks. So the monthly number is a planning tool. Not your bank deposit.

What is the formula for hourly to yearly?

Before tax: hourly rate × hours per week × 52 weeks. So $24 an hour at 40 hours a week is $24 × 40 × 52 = $49,920 a year. Want after-tax? Use the calculator above with your state.

Is $25 an hour good in the US?

It depends on where you live. $25 an hour is good in a low-cost city. Tight in a high-rent one. "Good" also depends on your family, your debt, and your savings goals. Not just your hourly rate. Use the tool above. See your take-home. Then check it against rent and bills.

How much rent can you afford on $25 an hour?

A common rule: aim for rent near 30% of your monthly take-home. Real budgets need some give. Since tax differs by state, two people at $25 an hour have different rent caps. Get your take-home first with the calculator above. Then multiply by 0.30. Adjust for debt and savings.

$30 an hour is how much per year?

At 40 hours a week, $30 an hour is about $62,400 a year before tax. ($30 × 40 × 52.) That's about $5,200 a month gross. Your take-home is less. It depends on your state. Run your numbers above for your real after-tax pay.

How much do taxes take from hourly pay?

Most US workers pay three taxes from their paycheck. Federal income tax. Social Security and Medicare (FICA). And often a state income tax. The total can run from 20% on lower pay to over 30% on high pay. Self-employed workers pay different rates. So 1099 income is not the same as W-2 hourly pay.

Our Salary Methodology, Data Sources & Editorial Standards

Last reviewed: May 26, 2026 · Reviewed by the Income Clarity editorial team · Read the full methodology

How We Calculate Take-Home Pay

  • Federal tax: IRS 2025 marginal brackets, single filer default, standard deduction applied.
  • FICA: 6.2% Social Security up to the $168,600 wage cap + 1.45% Medicare on all wages.
  • State tax: each state's 2025 published rates. Nine states modeled as zero (TX, FL, WA, etc.).
  • California adds SDI at 1.1% with no wage cap.

Data Sources

  • IRS Publication 15-T — federal withholding tables.
  • Social Security Administration — annual wage base.
  • IRS Tax Topic 751 — FICA rates.
  • Tax Foundation — state income tax brackets.

What This Isn't

A planning tool, not tax filing software. We don't model 401(k) deductions, HSA contributions, employer health premiums, or itemized deductions. Real take-home is usually within $300/month of our estimate.
