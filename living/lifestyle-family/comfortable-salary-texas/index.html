No state income tax: what it really saves you

Texas does not tax your wages. That alone adds about $4,800 a year to a $75,000 paycheck. Compared to California. At $150,000, the win nearly doubles.

What this means for you

The tax win gets bigger as your salary climbs. At entry pay, the gap is small. At six figures, you keep thousands more in Texas each year.

But don't count it twice. If you buy a $400,000 home, Texas property tax runs about $8,000 a year. That's roughly what California state tax costs at the same salary. Renters keep more of the win.

Property tax: the Texas catch

Texas funds schools through property tax. Not income tax. So owners pay a lot. Rates run from 1.5% to over 3% of home value. Depending on the county and school district.

What this means for you

On a $400,000 home, your property tax bill is bigger than most people's car payment. Budget $600 to $900 a month. On top of your mortgage.

Texas offers a homestead exemption. It knocks about $100,000 off the school district value of your primary home. That cuts a real chunk. Ask your county appraisal district before you close.

Is $80,000 a good salary in Texas?

It depends on the metro. And whether you have kids. $80,000 is a solid single income almost anywhere in Texas. But it's a tight family income in Austin and Dallas.

Cars and commutes: the Texas tax you forgot

Texas was built for cars. Most metros have weak transit. Most families own two cars. And most jobs need at least one.

Plan for these monthly car costs. Per car:

  • Payment: $400 to $700 for a typical used or new vehicle.
  • Insurance: $130 to $220. Higher in Houston and Dallas.
  • Gas: $180 to $300. A suburb commute eats more.
  • Repairs: $80 to $150. Averaged across the year.

That's about $800 to $1,400 per car per month. Two cars can run $1,800 to $2,800 a month. A quiet $20,000-a-year line item.

What this means for you

If your job goes remote, dropping the second car saves about $10,000 a year. That alone can move you up one budget tier. Without earning a dollar more.

5 ways to stretch a Texas paycheck

Each of these saves at least $1,000 a year. Stack two or three and you have a real raise.

Claim the homestead exemption

It cuts $500 to $1,500 a year off your property tax bill. File once with your county appraisal district when you buy.

Move to the suburbs

Plano, Frisco, Katy, and Round Rock often beat the urban core on rent. Just check the toll road and gas math first.

Drop the second car

If you can work remote, one car saves $700 to $1,200 a month. That's $8,000 to $14,000 a year.

Tame the AC bill

Texas summers crush utility budgets. A smart thermostat at 78 degrees saves $40 to $80 a month. From May to September.

Compare offers on take-home

Texas wins on no state tax. But benefits, 401(k) match, and property tax move the needle too. Always compare the full number.

Texas salary FAQ

What is a comfortable salary in Texas?

For a family of four: about $115,000 in Houston. $120,000 in Dallas. And $140,000 in Austin. A single person can live well on $60,000 to $80,000 in most metros.

Does Texas have state income tax?

No. Texas does not tax your wages. You still owe federal income tax and FICA. Sales tax and property tax are higher than average.

Why are Texas property taxes so high?

Texas funds schools through property tax. Not income tax. On a $400,000 home, expect $7,000 to $10,000 a year. That's $600 to $900 a month.

Austin vs Dallas: which needs more income?

Austin needs about $20,000 more. Austin rent has jumped fast. Dallas has more affordable suburbs. Like Plano and Frisco.

Is Texas cheaper than California?

Almost always, yes. For a family of four, the same lifestyle costs $40,000 to $80,000 less per year. The gap shrinks if you buy a home. Texas property tax is high.
