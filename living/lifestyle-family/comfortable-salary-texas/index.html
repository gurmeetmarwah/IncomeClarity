Texas salary FAQ

What is a comfortable salary in Texas?

Often $95k–$140k gross for a comfortable family of four in Dallas or Houston, and $110k–$155k+ in Austin—before premium homes or private school. Use your metro and tier in the planner.

Does Texas have state income tax?

No wage income tax at the state level. You still owe federal tax and payroll tax; sales and property taxes are relatively high.

Why are Texas property taxes so high?

Texas funds schools and local services heavily through property tax instead of state income tax. Owners should model $600–$900+/month on many family homes in major metros.

Austin vs Dallas income needs?

Austin rents and childcare have climbed quickly; Dallas–Fort Worth offers more suburban options at lower rent bands. Run both in the metro compare above.

Is Texas cheaper than California?

Often lower gross needs thanks to no state income tax and lower rent in many metros—but not always lower total cost. Compare your cities and own vs rent on both planners.
