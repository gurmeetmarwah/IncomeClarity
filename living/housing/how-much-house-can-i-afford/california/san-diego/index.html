The 28/36 rule in San Diego

Lenders often use two caps. Our calculator applies both so you see a realistic max — not just what a bank might pre-approve.

28% front-end

Your full housing payment — mortgage, tax, insurance, and HOA — should stay near or below 28% of gross monthly income. In San Diego, insurance and HOA often push buyers over this line before list price does.

36% back-end

All monthly debt plus housing should stay near or below 36% of gross income. A $400 car payment and $250 in student loans shrink your max home price even when income is strong.

Stress meter

We label results Comfortable, Moderate, Stretched, or Over limit. Shop below your max if you want room for repairs, childcare, or savings — especially in California where tax and insurance vary by block.
