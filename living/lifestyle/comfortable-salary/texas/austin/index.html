What to know about Austin

Local rent, tax, and daily costs drive how much you need to earn in Austin.

  • Median 1BR rent near $1,750/mo is usually the largest line in a comfort budget.
  • Cost-of-living index 108 (US average = 100) captures rent, food, utilities, and transport pressure.
  • Many singles plan around $82,000 gross here before childcare, debt, and extra savings goals.
  • Tax context: No state income tax; property tax is high for owners
