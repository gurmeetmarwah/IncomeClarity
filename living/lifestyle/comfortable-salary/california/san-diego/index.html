What “comfortable” means in San Diego

Comfort is not just paying rent on time. In our model, a comfortable San Diego salary covers median housing, groceries, utilities, and transport — plus 10–12% savings and modest lifestyle spending. Median rent near $2,550/month and California state income tax (up to 13.3%) both shape take-home.

San Diego sits at cost-of-living index 148 (US average = 100). Many singles target about $115,000 gross for a comfortable tier. Before you compare offers, convert gross to take-home in our hourly-to-salary after-tax calculator and check rent against our 28% rule.

What to know about San Diego

Local rent, tax, and daily costs drive how much you need to earn in San Diego.

  • Median 1BR rent near $2,550/mo is usually the largest line in a comfort budget.
  • Cost-of-living index 148 (US average = 100) captures rent, food, utilities, and transport pressure.
  • Many singles plan around $102,000 gross here before childcare, debt, and extra savings goals.
  • Tax context: State income tax up to 13.3% on high earners
