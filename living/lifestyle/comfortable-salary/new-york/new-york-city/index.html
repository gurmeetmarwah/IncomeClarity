What “comfortable” means in New York City

Comfort is not just paying rent on time. In our model, a comfortable NYC salary covers median housing, groceries, utilities, and transport — plus 10–12% savings and modest lifestyle spending. Median rent near $3,400/month and state plus city income tax both compress take-home.

New York City sits at cost-of-living index 158 (US average = 100). Many singles target about $140,000 gross for a comfortable tier. Before you compare offers, convert gross to take-home in our hourly-to-salary after-tax calculator and check rent against our 28% rule.

What to know about New York City

Local rent, tax, and daily costs drive how much you need to earn in New York City.

  • Median 1BR rent near $3,400/mo is usually the largest line in a comfort budget.
  • Cost-of-living index 158 (US average = 100) captures rent, food, utilities, and transport pressure.
  • Many singles plan around $120,000 gross here before childcare, debt, and extra savings goals.
  • Tax context: State + NYC city income tax for many downstate workers
