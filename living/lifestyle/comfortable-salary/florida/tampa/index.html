What to know about Tampa

Local rent, tax, and daily costs drive how much you need to earn in Tampa.

  • Median 1BR rent near $1,650/mo is usually the largest line in a comfort budget.
  • Cost-of-living index 106 (US average = 100) captures rent, food, utilities, and transport pressure.
  • Many singles plan around $74,000 gross here before childcare, debt, and extra savings goals.
  • Tax context: No state income tax; insurance can be high on the coast
