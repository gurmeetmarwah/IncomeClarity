Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to pay off $5k debt?

With 22% APR and $200/month, many models land near 2–3 years and roughly $1,400 in interest. Your issuer’s minimum formula, new charges, and exact APR will differ—use the planner above with your numbers.

How much interest will I pay on $5,000?

Total interest equals all finance charges until the balance hits zero. Higher monthly payments and lower APR reduce it. The comparison table shows interest at several payment levels.

Is debt snowball or avalanche better?

For a single $5,000 balance, both methods point to the same account. With multiple debts, avalanche minimizes interest; snowball clears small balances first for motivation. See our payoff strategies hub.

Should I make extra payments?

If your budget allows, yes—extra principal early usually cuts months and total interest. Model +$50 or +$100 in the “What if” section before committing.

What is a realistic payoff timeline?

Many households target 18–36 months with steady payments above the minimum. Match payment size to take-home pay after rent, food, and insurance—not just the lender’s minimum.
