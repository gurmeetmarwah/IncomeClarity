Frequently asked questions

Is it worth paying debt off early?

Yes, on high-rate cards. Extra pay cuts interest and moves up your debt-free date. Run your numbers in the calculator above.

Should I save or pay off debt?

Many people keep a small cash buffer and still pay extra on high-rate cards. If your card rate beats your savings rate, debt paydown often wins first.

Is debt snowball or avalanche better?

Avalanche saves more interest. Snowball clears small balances first for motivation. The better pick is the one you will follow every month.

How much extra should I pay?

Pick an amount you can pay every month. Often $25 to $100 helps. Tap the cards above to see time and interest saved.

How much interest can I save?

It depends on your balance, rate, and extra pay. The results panel shows dollars saved vs your base payment.
