❓ Popular questions

Credit Card Debt FAQ

How much should I pay toward my credit card each month?

At minimum, pay more than the monthly interest charge. A fixed payment above that line clears the balance faster. Use our payoff calculator to find a sustainable amount for your budget.

How long does it take to pay off credit card debt?

It depends on balance, APR, and payment size. Browse payoff scenarios by balance or run your exact numbers in the payoff calculator.

What happens if I only pay the minimum?

Most of each minimum goes to interest. Your balance shrinks slowly and total interest can exceed the original debt. Read what happens if you only pay the minimum.

Is a balance transfer worth it?

It can be if you pay off during the promo window and fees do not erase savings. Compare options in balance transfer vs paying interest.

What is a good credit card APR?

Under 20% is strong for prime borrowers; many US cards run 22–24%+. See what APR means and prioritize paying down the highest-rate balances first.
