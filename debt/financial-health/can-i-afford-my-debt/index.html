      🔒 How we built this

      Debt affordability methodology

      Last reviewed: June 2026 · Full methodology · Editorial policy · Calculator methodology

      How we score

      • Debt payments ÷ take-home pay (28% weight)
      • Housing + debt ÷ take-home (22%)
      • Emergency fund months (18%)
      • Monthly flexibility (17%)
      • Gross DTI vs 36% lender cap (15%)

      What this isn't

      Not loan pre-approval or credit counseling. Necessities are estimated from household size and location — adjust with your real budget.

      US-only tools to calculate your real income after tax, understand debt, and make smarter financial decisions.
