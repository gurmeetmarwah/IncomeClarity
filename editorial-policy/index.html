Why Income Clarity exists

Most money sites show gross pay or vague tips. We build free US calculators that answer one real question at a time. What is my take-home pay? How long until I am debt-free? Should I rent or buy? Is a 1099 contract worth it?

Every page is educational. We are not a lender, broker, or tax preparer. See about us for who runs the site.

Who creates and reviews content

Income Clarity is built by a small US-focused editorial team. We specialize in:

  • Take-home pay and payroll taxes — federal, FICA, and state withholding
  • Consumer debt — APR, payoff timelines, DTI, and affordability
  • Housing costs — rent vs buy, affordability rules, and metro benchmarks
  • Freelance vs W-2 pay — self-employment tax and benefits trade-offs

Humans verify every public number against a primary source before publish. AI tools may help with drafts or code. They do not replace editorial review.

Editorial process

  1. Define the user question. Each page solves one problem, not a keyword list.
  2. Document the math. Calculator logic is written in calculator methodology before or with launch.
  3. Cite primary sources. IRS, SSA, Federal Reserve, BLS, CFPB, Freddie Mac, and similar agencies — not press summaries alone.
  4. Plain-language edit. Short sentences. No jargon walls. Formulas shown where they matter.
  5. Scheduled review. Tax tables, caps, and rates are checked at least yearly. Pages show a Last reviewed date.

Sources and fact-checking

We do not use paid “industry insight” feeds for core numbers. When we cite a statistic, we link to the agency or dataset behind it. Full source list: methodology data sources.

Before a calculator ships, we test sample outputs against spreadsheets or published examples. Take-home results are spot-checked against real pay stubs when possible.

Content review schedule

  • Tax and payroll tools — reviewed when IRS and SSA publish annual updates (typically Q4–Q1).
  • Debt and APR guides — reviewed when Fed household debt reports or major rate shifts warrant it.
  • Housing and COL pages — reviewed when Zillow, BLS, or Census publish annual rebases.
  • All pages — carry Last reviewed: June 2026 in the footer unless a page was updated more recently.

Use of AI

We may use AI to speed up first drafts, outline pages, or generate boilerplate code. A human editor checks facts, sources, and calculator output before anything goes live. AI does not pick final tax rates, APR defaults, or affordability rules.

Corrections policy

If you find an error, contact us with the URL and what looks wrong. A primary source helps.

  1. We confirm the issue within 7 business days when possible.
  2. We fix confirmed errors and update the page’s review date.
  3. Material formula changes are noted on the relevant methodology page.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data or run pay-per-lead funnels to lenders.
  • We do not take affiliate fees from card issuers or mortgage lenders for calculator rankings.
  • We do not provide personalized tax, legal, or investment advice. See terms and privacy policy.
