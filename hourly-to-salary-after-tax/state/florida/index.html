Is $25 an hour enough in Florida?

$25 an hour in Florida pays about $3,650 a month after tax. Inland Florida works. The coast is tight. Rent, storm insurance, and gas eat the budget fast.

What this means for you

Florida home insurance now runs $4,000 to $7,000 a year in many coastal counties. That can erase the $3,000 you save on state income tax.

The no-tax win is real for renters and inland buyers. For coastal homeowners, run the insurance numbers first. Don't celebrate yet.

Housing

Rent is highest in South Florida. And coastal retiree areas. Miami-Dade, Broward, and Naples need bigger incomes for the same life as inland counties.

Taxes

No state wage tax. But local sales tax and property tax still matter. Homestead status can cut your housing bill if you live there full time.

Home insurance

Storm risk is the big Florida cost. Home insurance, flood coverage, and high deductibles can wipe out the no-tax win in risky ZIP codes.

City matters

$25 an hour stretches in Jacksonville or Tallahassee. It gets tight in Miami. The gap is bigger than new arrivals expect.

Why people move to Florida for income

Florida draws movers for three reasons. No state tax. Retiree-friendly rules. And warm weather. But insurance can erase the tax win.

No state tax on wages

Florida workers keep more of each check. No state tax on wage income. Just federal tax and FICA.

Retirees and remote workers

Many retirees and remote workers move here for the tax cuts and sun. That demand pushes up rent and home prices. Especially in Tampa and Orlando.

Insurance is a big line

Home and flood insurance can be huge in Florida. In coastal ZIPs, those costs can match or beat what you save on state tax.

Florida tax and lifestyle FAQ

Common questions on Florida wage tax, the retiree pull, and how insurance reshapes affordability.

Does Florida tax my paycheck?

No. Florida has no state tax on wages. Your paycheck is just federal tax and FICA. Same as Texas, Washington, or Nevada.

Why do retirees move to Florida?

No state income tax. No estate tax. And no tax on Social Security or pensions. Plus warm weather. For a retiree on $80,000 a year, the savings can be $4,000 or more per year. Over a high-tax state.

How much does insurance hurt Florida affordability?

A lot. Home insurance now runs $4,000 to $7,000 a year in many coastal counties. That can wipe out the $3,000 to $5,000 you save on state tax.

How much is $30 an hour after taxes in Florida?

$30 an hour at 40 hours a week is about $62,400 gross. With no state tax, take-home is near $4,340 a month. Use the calculator above for your real numbers.

Florida Salary Methodology, Data Sources & Editorial Standards

Last reviewed: May 26, 2026 · Reviewed by the Income Clarity editorial team · Read the full methodology

How We Calculate FL Take-Home

  • Federal tax: IRS 2025 brackets + standard deduction.
  • FICA: 6.2% Social Security to $168,600 wage cap + 1.45% Medicare.
  • No state income tax — wages are taxed only by the federal government and FICA.
  • Higher homeowners insurance in coastal counties (hurricane risk).
  • Sales tax 6% state + up to 2% local; no local wage tax.

Data Sources

  • IRS Publication 15-T — federal withholding tables.
  • Social Security Administration — annual wage base.
  • Florida Department of Revenue.

What This Isn't

A FL tax return preparer. We don't model 401(k) pretax deductions, employer health premiums, HSA contributions, or itemized deductions. Real take-home is usually within $300/month of our estimate.
