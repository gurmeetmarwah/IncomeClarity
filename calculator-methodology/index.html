Take-home pay calculator

Take-home = Gross − Federal tax − FICA − State tax − SDI (where applicable)

  • Federal: IRS marginal brackets + standard deduction for filing status.
  • FICA: 6.2% Social Security to wage base, 1.45% Medicare, +0.9% additional Medicare above thresholds.
  • State: published brackets; zero for no-income-tax states.
  • Excludes: 401(k), health premiums, HSA — use your pay stub to compare.

Credit card & debt payoff calculators

Each month: interest = balance × (APR ÷ 12); principal = payment − interest

  • Monthly periodic rate from nominal APR.
  • Minimum payment illustrations often use max($25, 2% of balance).
  • Extra payments reduce months and total interest linearly in the model.
  • Snowball vs avalanche: multi-card simulations pay minimums first, then target order.

Daily interest calculator

Daily rate = APR ÷ 365 · Simple: interest = principal × daily rate × days

Compound: ending = principal × (1 + APR÷365÷100)^days

  • Simple mode: interest does not reinvest.
  • Daily compounding: matches many US card and savings accrual patterns.
  • Does not model fees, grace periods, or issuer-specific day-count conventions.

Debt affordability & DTI tools

  • DTI: total monthly debt payments ÷ gross monthly income.
  • Take-home debt ratio: debt payments ÷ monthly take-home pay.
  • Debt health score: weighted blend of DTI, housing+debt share, emergency months, and cash-flow flexibility.
  • Breathing room: take-home minus housing, debt, and estimated necessities by household size.

Rent vs buy calculator

Multi-year total cost comparison — not a net-worth projection.

  • Rent grows at user rate (default ~3.5%/yr).
  • Buy side: 30-year amortization, property tax, insurance, maintenance, appreciation, closing costs.
  • Opportunity cost on down payment at user-assumed return (default 5%).

1099 vs W-2 calculator

  • Self-employment tax 15.3% on 92.35% of net earnings (Schedule SE).
  • 50% of SE tax deducted before income tax.
  • Health premium added to contractor costs; 401(k) match optional on W-2 side.

Rent & house affordability

  • 28% rule: housing ≤ 28% of gross income.
  • 28/36 rule: housing ≤ 28%, total debt ≤ 36% of gross.
  • Default mortgage rate near Freddie Mac PMMS; 20% down unless changed.
