Frequently asked questions

What is Income Clarity?

Income Clarity is a set of free, US-focused calculators that translate wages, debt, housing, and freelance income into estimates—especially after taxes—so you can compare options before you commit.

Is Income Clarity free?

Yes. Every tool runs in your browser with no account or paywall.

Which calculator should I use first?

For hourly or salaried take-home by state, use Income. For card balances, use Debt. For housing, use Living. For contractor vs employee pay, use Freelance.

How accurate are the estimates?

They are planning estimates using simplified tax and loan models. Real life varies by employer withholding, lender fees, rent rules, and tax law—verify big decisions with a CPA or advisor.

Do these calculators cover all US states?

Income and freelance flows include state selection for tax estimates. Other tools rely on your inputs and US-style assumptions; always check local rules for your situation.
